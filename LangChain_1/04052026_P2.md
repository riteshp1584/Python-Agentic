**Title**

Google Generative AI – Story Generation and Mood Analysis

**Overview**

This script demonstrates how to build a multi-step LangChain pipeline with Google Generative AI (Gemini API). The first chain generates a short story based on a topic, and the second chain analyzes the mood of that story. By combining chains, the workflow showcases how to perform generation → analysis in sequence using the gemini-2.5-flash model.

**Key Features**

📝 Story Generation: Creates a short story from a given topic.

🎭 Mood Analysis: Evaluates the emotional tone of the generated story.

🔗 Chained Workflow: Connects multiple chains into a single pipeline.

🤖 LLM Integration: Uses ChatGoogleGenerativeAI with Gemini 2.5 Flash.

✅ Concise Output: Produces both creative and analytical results.

**Why It’s Useful**

✅ Demonstrates multi-step reasoning with Gemini.

✅ Shows how to chain creative generation and analytical evaluation.

✅ Useful for building pipelines that combine content creation + sentiment analysis.

✅ Serves as a foundation for advanced workflows (e.g., storytelling, report generation, content review).
